About the group

Sisters, singing since they can remember

Alive Vocal Group is a sister trio from Rich Square, North Carolina — Jessica Jefferson, Amanda King, and Emily King — who have been singing together for 20 years. Their sound moves effortlessly across genres: rock, country, R&B, pop, gospel, and everything in between.
